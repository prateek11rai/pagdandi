Kedarkantha केदारकांठा

Uttarakhand · Govind Wildlife Sanctuary, Mori tehsil, Uttarkashi district
Days3
Max alt3,810 m
SeasonApr–Jun, Sep–Nov
DifficultyModerate
Budget (est.)₹6,000–12,000
Distance20 km weak source
This trek goes to 3,810 m. Above about 3,500 m, altitude sickness becomes more likely. Climb gradually, drink water, and turn back if headache, nausea or breathlessness get worse. Not medical advice, and not a substitute for a licensed local guide.

What you'll see

  • A full-circle summit panorama: Swargarohini, Bandarpunch and Black Peak.
  • Juda Ka Talab, a small high lake used as a campsite.
  • Pine, deodar, oak and rhododendron forest.

About and data

What Pagdandi is

An open database of Indian treks, with a guide you can ask. Tell it about your group and it recommends treks it can back with sources. Then it gets out of the way: an audio intro for the drive, a one-page plan for the trail.

How the guide works

The guide is Gemma 4, an open-weight model we host ourselves. It only recommends treks from this database, every number on a card comes from the database, and plain code checks the numbers in its sentences before you see them. Chats aren't stored.

Where the data comes from

  • 20 treks, one file each, written from Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap, UNESCO and government pages. No trekking companies.
  • Every fact names its source. If we couldn't find one, the fact is blank and the page says "not known yet". A fact we could only source weakly is marked.
  • Trek text is CC BY-SA 4.0. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Forecasts from Open-Meteo. Anyone can fix a trek or add one on GitHub.

Not advice

Pagdandi is not medical advice and not a substitute for a licensed local guide. Read the official advisories before you go.
